HIGH PRIORITY // HIGH-WATER OR AIR LOSS Daniel's Construction • (337) 684-5394

Red Alarm Light & Buzzer Sounding on ATU Control Box

An audible buzzer and red light on your Delta, AeroGreen, or aerobic control panel indicates either a High-Water condition in the tank or Low Air Pressure from the linear compressor.

Immediate Safe Checks & Action Plan:
  1. Flip the "MUTE / SILENCE" toggle switch on the side or face of the control box to stop the buzzer while keeping the visual red light active.
  2. Listen near the aerator box: do you hear the steady hum of the air compressor? If silent, check your home breaker panel for a tripped breaker.
  3. If the compressor is running, check whether heavy South Louisiana rain has saturated the drainfield/discharge area or if the effluent pump float is stuck.
  4. Reduce household water usage (pause laundry and dishwasher loads) and call Daniel's Construction at (337) 684-5394 for aerator, float, or pump service.

How Septic Permitting Works in Acadia & Surrounding Parishes

Under Louisiana Sanitary Code Title 51, Part XIII, every new home or mobile home outside municipal city sewer requires a two-stage Parish Sanitarian permit (Form LHS-47) before your electric company can connect permanent power.

STEP 01 // TEMPORARY PERMIT (LHS-47)

Site Evaluation & Power Release

Submit your site layout and property dimensions to your Parish Health Unit Sanitarian. Once your temporary sewage permit (Form LHS-47) is issued, your parish permit office and utility provider (SLEMCO, Entergy, Cleco) release your building and temporary power permits.

STEP 02 // LICENSED INSTALLATION

Installed by Daniel's Construction

Louisiana law requires installation by a state-licensed installer. Under Basic License #30733 and Combination License #183, Daniel's Construction excavates, sets your in-house precast concrete or Delta/AeroGreen ATU tank, and trenches your effluent system.

STEP 03 // FINAL SANITARIAN SIGN-OFF

Open-Trench Inspection & Final Permit

Before final backfill cover, the Parish Sanitarian verifies system elevation, electrical connection, and manufacturer compliance, issuing your Final LHS-47 Approval for permanent occupancy.
